## About the company

**思考者咖啡有限公司** is a human-centered AI company founded by Cruz Tang／湯明軒. It builds ThinkerCafe and HumanOS so AI first understands a person's context, obtains that person's consent, and only then helps organize, act, and review outcomes.

The company is currently operated by its founder as a one-person company. There is no current team, no open position, and no public storefront. This GitHub organization is the engineering and open-source entry point; it does not replace the full parent-company identity.

## Brand and product architecture

- **ThinkerCafe** — the people-facing product and managed service. People can leave context by voice or text without organizing the problem first. AI processing and Cruz's personal review must be presented as separate states.
- **HumanOS** — a model-neutral, forkable open-source protocol/kernel for consent, candidate actions, outcome review, protocols, and rollback. It is currently an engineering candidate undergoing open-source qualification validation; it does not claim proven life improvement or authority to manage a person.
- **CruzOS** — Cruz's private HumanOS fork and internal intelligence qualification. It is not a first-visit product, and private context is not published.
- **苗栗好朋友** — a Miaoli local service developed by the same company. Its public identity, use context, data and memory, money flows, and external commitments remain separate.
- **Podcast and media** — the company's content and build-in-public layer, documenting AI in real life, HumanOS research, and one-person-company experiments. It is not the company's entire identity.

Belonging to the same company does not make identity, content, consent, data, memory, money flows, or commitments transfer automatically between services.
